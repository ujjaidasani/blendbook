# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- **Download and open**: a Mac app (.dmg) and a Windows installer, with Python and everything else built in.
- **Built for dance music and techno**, on rekordbox 6 and 7.
- **rekordbox first**: tempo and key come from rekordbox's own analysis, and energy from its waveforms for every analysed track, streamed ones included, with no internet. Online data only fills gaps.
- **Opens on Plan**, or on Live if a set is running. Plan builds a set from blends you've played before, and Plan and Insights lead with your taste: what you're leaning on, forgotten favourites, and what you've never played out.
- **Find new music**: similar artists from MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz, and songs and 30-second previews from Apple. Digs keep to your plan's sound, skip artists MusicBrainz places away from the club and household names far better known than anyone in your plan, give any one artist at most three tracks, take a minute or two the first time because Apple allows about 20 lookups a minute, and show their progress. Every track links to Apple Music, Spotify and Beatport.
- **Only rekordbox keys count**: harmonic picks and clash warnings don't use online keys, which matched rekordbox only 28% of the time. They're shown as estimates (≈8A), and the **Keys card** shows how many tracks still need analysing in rekordbox, and how.
- **Any controller**: the DDJ-FLX4 and DDJ-400 work right away; teach Blendbook any other MIDI controller in about a minute.
- **The built-in coach answers what you ask**, with no API key: what's next, lift it or bring it down, keys, tempo, time left, how the set's going, the last blend, how to get out of this track, and your plan.
- **Live keeps up** while hidden behind rekordbox, lets a hand-picked track give way to the next logged one, doesn't read a long outro as the set ending, and isn't held up by recording analysis.
- **Listen** measures levels. rekordbox doesn't allow recording streamed tracks, so Blendbook doesn't save audio from the page, but it analyses rekordbox's REC and dropped-in recordings.
- **Settings**: choose your rekordbox folder if it isn't in the usual place, copy diagnostics for bug reports, and turn on an optional daily check for new versions. A clear message if a rekordbox update stops Blendbook reading your library.
- **Quick with big libraries**: tested with 26,000 tracks and 1,500 sets, where Sessions and Insights open in under a third of a second and a 12-track plan builds in about 3 seconds.
- First-run guidance, and a menu bar / tray icon to reopen or quit.
- Your data lives in your own user folder, separate from the app, so updates can't touch it.
